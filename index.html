        Your sugar sources

        Choose a source on each logged treat using its ingredients. Amounts group each food’s logged sugar by your selection; they do not measure individual sugar molecules. Use “Mixed” when a food contains more than one source.

        What do these types mean?

        Refined added: table sugar, cane sugar, dextrose, or corn syrup. Honey, maple, and agave are added sugars too. Naturally occurring: sugar within plain fruit or milk. “Processed” is not a separate sugar type. Ingredients identify sources, but usually cannot tell you the grams of each. Presets and older logs start as unknown.
